FONTLOG for Sunday Serif
------------------------

This file provides detailed information on the Sunday Serif font software.
This information should be distributed along with the Sunday Serif fonts
and any derivative works.


Basic Font Information
----------------------

Sunday Serif is a soft serif for slow days, made for Sunday
(https://joinsunday.co). It comes in roman and italic, with a weight axis from Regular (400) to SemiBold (600) and three effect axes:

- Light Burn (BURN, -100 to 100): like a photo exposure. Towards -100 the
  ink spreads and fills the letters; towards +100 the thin strokes burn
  away until they break, and some letters switch to broken-line drawings.
- Ink Bleed (INKB, 0 to 100): ink soaking into paper, fatter and rounder
  letters with slightly tighter spacing.
- Worn Print (WORN, 0 to 100): eroded edges and small holes in the ink,
  like a worn letterpress.

The outlines are derived from Source Serif 4 by Adobe (SIL Open Font
License 1.1, Reserved Font Name "Source"), reworked in shape, proportions,
spacing and character set. The character set covers Basic Latin,
Latin-1 Supplement and Latin Extended-A.

Sources are UFO masters with two designspace files, built with fontmake.
See README.md for the build.
